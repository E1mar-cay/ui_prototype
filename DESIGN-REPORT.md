# Responsive UI Prototype — Design Rationale

**Scenario:** Northform Supply product-detail page for The Everyday Pack, a 20-liter everyday backpack.

The mobile layout is the starting point: a single-column product story keeps the product artwork, name, price, color choice, and purchase action in a clear reading order. I kept secondary details in expandable sections so shoppers can scan the essentials without losing access to specifications, care information, or reviews. A persistent purchase bar keeps the price and primary action in the lower thumb zone while scrolling. The menu, color swatches, view selectors, and wishlist controls have touch-friendly hit areas, and the purchase bar respects the device safe area.

At tablet widths, the page becomes a two-column composition: the product gallery occupies slightly more than half the content area, while product information remains visible beside it. Navigation expands from a compact menu into inline links, and the purchase action moves into the product details to make better use of the larger viewport. The gallery and text retain a comfortable gap rather than stretching to the screen edges.

At desktop widths, a wider centered canvas gives the gallery and product details more breathing room. Larger product typography, a complete navigation bar, and more generous spacing establish a stronger visual hierarchy without changing the order or meaning of the content. The same muted palette, type scale, and button styling are used at every breakpoint for consistency.

The visual direction pairs warm paper tones, moss green, and a small brass accent with a custom backpack illustration. This keeps attention on the product while making the page feel calm and outdoors-oriented. Mobile-specific considerations include readable type, visible stock and shipping information, large controls, keyboard focus states, semantic labels, and reduced-motion support. The prototype also includes working color selection, gallery view controls, expandable product details, wishlist feedback, and an add-to-bag count. Together, the three layouts demonstrate responsive adaptation while preserving a consistent shopping flow.
